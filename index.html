04 / About

Five years
compressed

Self-taught from age fifteen. Built a client base from zero. Now editing for top creators, brands, and anything that demands precision.

Motion design
obsession.
No safety net.

I'm Federico Micale — motion designer and video editor from Milan, Italy. Picked up After Effects at fifteen and never put it down. No design school, no safety net. Five years of making things, breaking things, making them better.

Good motion isn't decoration — it's argument. Every cut, every easing curve, every pause is a decision about what the viewer should feel next. I've spent five years learning which decisions stick and which slide off. That's the work under the work.

I've edited for Italy's biggest YouTubers and TikTokers — five million views on YouTube, two hundred million on TikTok — and delivered branded work for Revolut. I build in After Effects, finish in Premiere and DaVinci, and use AI where it makes the work faster, not lazier.

Why me? Because I'm early. Nineteen with the reps of a mid-level designer and the hunger of someone who still has everything to prove. Deadlines sharpen the focus — that's where the best work lives.

Who is Federico Micale?

Federico Micale, known online as FedeBuilds, is a motion designer and video editor based in Milan, Italy. With five years of experience since 2020, he creates brand films, product explainers, and editorial cuts for top Italian YouTubers, TikTokers, and brands including Revolut.

What services does FedeBuilds offer?

FedeBuilds offers six core disciplines: motion graphics, video editing, kinetic typography, UI and product motion design, color grading and finishing, and AI-assisted production workflows. Tools include After Effects, Cinema 4D, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Figma, and Runway.

Federico believes good motion is argument, not decoration. Every cut, easing curve, and pause is a deliberate decision about what the viewer should feel next. He prioritizes story-first pacing, considered transitions, and the final 10% of polish that separates cinematic work from amateur output.
